Frequently Asked Questions

Everything customers usually ask us — before they ask us.

Typical transit times from Ernakulam: Gulf countries 2–4 working days, UK/Europe 4–6 days, USA/Canada 5–8 days, Australia/NZ 5–8 days. Customs inspection at the destination can add 1–2 days. See our Destinations page for the full list.
You pay for the chargeable weight — the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight. Volumetric weight = Length × Width × Height (in cm) ÷ 5000. A big light box can therefore cost more than a small heavy one. We always weigh and measure in front of you and explain the calculation before you pay.
Yes — free doorstep pickup across Ernakulam district including Alangad, Aluva, Paravur, Kalamassery, Perumbavoor, Angamaly and nearby areas. For urgent same-day pickups, call or WhatsApp us before noon.
For personal shipments: sender's Aadhaar/passport copy, the receiver's full address and phone number, and a simple list of contents with approximate values. For medicines: a doctor's prescription. For commercial shipments: GST invoice. We prepare all the shipping paperwork (AWB and customs declaration) for you.
Basic carrier liability is included on every shipment. For high-value items, additional shipment protection can be added at booking for a small percentage of the declared value — we recommend it for anything valuable or fragile.

If the shipment value is within the destination's duty-free limit, usually nothing. Above that, the destination customs may charge import duty/VAT which the receiver pays before delivery. We'll advise you of the likely charges for your destination before you ship.
