# Implementation 04 — Future Enhancements 🔮 PLANNED

Ideas in rough priority order. None block the current live site.

## 1. Custom Domain
- Suggested: `skywaycargo.in` (~₹500–800/year from GoDaddy/Hostinger/BigRock)
- Points at the existing GitHub Pages site via CNAME — zero code changes needed
- Looks professional on visiting cards; required for a business email like info@skywaycargo.in

## 2. Google Business Profile
- Claim/create the profile for "SKYWAY Air Cargo and Courier Services", Alangad
- Add the website URL, photos, hours (24×7), and services
- Biggest single driver of local "courier near me" traffic; reviews feed the site's trust story

## 3. Search Console & Sitemap
- Add `sitemap.xml` (10 URLs) and `robots.txt`
- Submit to Google Search Console; monitor "cargo to UAE from Kochi"-style queries

## 4. Per-Country SEO Landing Pages
- Dedicated pages: "Cargo to UAE from Kochi", "Courier to UK from Ernakulam", etc.
- Each: rates table, transit time, allowed/restricted items for that country, FAQs
- These match exactly what people type into Google — high-intent traffic

## 5. Malayalam Language Toggle
- Large share of target customers are more comfortable in Malayalam
- Simple approach: `index-ml.html` duplicates with a language switch in the top bar

## 6. Real Review Embeds
- Replace the static Google reviews banner with a live embed (Elfsight/Shapo free tier, or manual monthly refresh of quotes)

## 7. WhatsApp Business Catalogue
- Set up WhatsApp Business with quick replies and a service catalogue; the site's wa.me links then land on a rich business profile

## 8. Analytics
- Add a lightweight analytics snippet (Google Analytics 4 or Plausible) to measure which pages and buttons convert
