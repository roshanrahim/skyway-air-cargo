Our Story

Local Team. Global Network.

SKYWAY Air Cargo and Courier Services was founded in 2021 in Alangad, Ernakulam with a simple goal: make international shipping easy, honest and affordable for the families and businesses of Kerala.

As an authorised agent of Aramex India Pvt and UPS, we combine the personal care of a local business with the reach of one of the world's largest logistics networks — more than 100 countries and territories.

Whether it's a certificate for a student abroad, a box of homemade snacks for family in the Gulf, or weekly commercial consignments for your business — we treat every shipment like it's our own.
