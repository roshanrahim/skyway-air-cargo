Prohibited & Restricted Items

Check this list before you pack — it saves time, money and customs trouble. When in doubt, WhatsApp us a photo of the item.

🚫 Never Allowed (Prohibited)

  • ✕Currency, gold, jewellery & precious stones — cash, bullion and high-value valuables cannot be couriered.
  • ✕Weapons, ammunition & explosives — including replicas, fireworks and crackers.
  • ✕Flammables & hazardous goods — lighters, gas cylinders, paints, chemicals, corrosives.
  • ✕Narcotics & contraband — all illegal drugs and psychotropic substances.
  • ✕Loose lithium batteries & power banks — airline dangerous-goods rules prohibit them in cargo.
  • ✕Electronics with built-in batteries — phones, laptops and other devices with lithium batteries cannot be shipped.
  • ✕Perfume — flammable; cannot be carried by air cargo in any quantity.
  • ✕Knives, scissors & sharp objects — blades and sharp tools of any kind.
  • ✕Aerosols & pressurised cans — deodorant sprays, shaving foam, etc.
  • ✕Perishable meat, fish & live animals/plants — including seeds and soil to most countries.
  • ✕Alcohol & tobacco — prohibited or heavily restricted to most destinations, incl. all Gulf countries.
  • ✕Pornographic or offensive material — strictly prohibited, especially to Gulf countries.
  • ✕Counterfeit goods — fake branded items are seized at customs.

⚠️ Allowed With Conditions (Restricted)

  • !Medicines — need a doctor's prescription in the receiver's or sender's name; payment invoice required and only up to 3 months' quantity allowed.
  • !Food items — packaged/dry foods with FSSAI certification generally OK; each country has its own rules (e.g. no homemade meat products). Ask us first.
  • !Ayurvedic products & herbal items — need ingredient labels, manufacturing details and date; some destinations require import permits.
  • !Goods in commercial quantity — commercial shipment documentation assistance provided.

✅ Commonly Shipped & Safe

  • ✓Documents, certificates & books
  • ✓Clothing, garments & footwear
  • ✓Dry food: snacks, spices, rice, tea with FSSAI certification (destination rules apply)
  • ✓Toys, gifts & handicrafts
  • ✓Household items & utensils
  • ✓Medicines (with prescription & invoice)
  • ✓Sport items
  • ✓Food samples
  • ✓Machine parts
Rules differ by destination country. This list is a general guide — the final word is always the destination country's customs regulations and Aramex's dangerous-goods policy. Message us on WhatsApp with the item and destination and we'll confirm before you pack.
